# Credits registry

The welcome panel and Credits & supporters dialog share the local registry in
`static/app-credits.js`. No remote requests, donor lookup or payment integration
are needed to display these names. Nothing has been published for 0.5.2 yet.

## Categories

* Community Contributors: useful testing, bug reports and suggestions.
* Diamond Contributors: exceptional, sustained feedback and testing.
* Supporters: financial support, currently the basic tier.
* Diamond Supporters: financial support exceeding EUR 100 in total.

The EUR 100 boundary is kept in the basic tier (up to and including EUR 100).
Amounts are not displayed or stored in the public registry. These are manual
recognition tiers, not automatic payment/account verification or paid features.
Community tiers depend on the value and continuity of a contribution, not merely
the number of reports. Recognition does not imply someone authored code.

## Display names

Use only the supplied public handle or approved display name. Stefano A. remains
abbreviated. Do not infer full names from payment details. Any later request to
change, expand or remove a name must be reflected in this single registry.
Social mentions require their own permission; the app does not publish posts.

## Interface

Credits are visible directly in the welcome panel, with the original model,
privacy and acceleration guidance retained. The existing Don't show this again
choice is respected. Credits remain available from File and Settings & about.
Opening full credits above welcome/settings preserves the underlying dialog and
restores focus when closed. Names never enter saved projects or video exports.
English is the default; Italian and light/dark themes are supported. The subtle
Diamond shimmer runs only on hover and honours reduced-motion preferences.
