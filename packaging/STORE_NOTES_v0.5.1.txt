Phrasync 0.5.1

Improved recovery for exports that could stall with footage, particles and 3D lyrics. Bounded encoder waits and complete-pass fallback preserve the composition. Fixed black image/video backgrounds in some 3D exports.

Export now clearly explains that editing is temporarily unavailable. Wait or cancel; controls recover after completion, failure or cancellation. Removed browser message boxes from app confirmations.

Background images and looping videos are editable timeline clips. Shared zoom, pan, fit-selection and IN/OUT navigation work across lanes. Higher tracks appear above lower tracks.

Four FX lanes let you place individual drops, pulses, light leaks and other effects, with intensity, fades and beat/transient/audio timing. Footage gradients sit beneath text.

Rain, snow, fog, storms and leaves work with Ocean, other worlds, images and videos. Added intensity, wind, speed and lightning controls. Precipitation follows world-space 3D camera movement.

Clearer model choices and Generate lyrics/subtitles controls, with generation and download states. Improved custom-font retry and protection from delayed results overwriting another project or newer edits.

Reduced decoder churn and retained GPU resources in complex timelines. Tested with 350 automated Windows checks and real-project exports, including 4K, frame rates, layered footage and cancellation.
