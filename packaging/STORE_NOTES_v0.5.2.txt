Phrasync 0.5.2 makes the editor easier to use on smaller screens.

The lyrics panel now scrolls from editing tools to the last phrase. In shorter windows, the paste panel starts collapsed so more lyrics are visible. Switch between a centered timeline, with full-height side panels, and a full-width timeline. Your choice and panel sizes are saved.

Colored handles resize Studio, Lyrics and the timeline with a mouse or keyboard. Expanding the timeline scales the video preview while preserving its aspect ratio.

Community Contributors and Supporters are now recognized in the welcome screen and in Credits & supporters, available from File and Settings.
